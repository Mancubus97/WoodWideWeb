Root tips produce extremely faint electrical fields.
Tree roots release strigolactones and other exudates, and fungi respond by:

Slowing the main tip

Increasing lateral branching

Forming a dense bushy network around the root


Low nutrients = more branching (search strategy)

Gameplay Ideas:

- Trees can grow
- Trees have a limit to which they can grow
- fungal network has limited storage capacity
- Player can place trees, player cant control the fungi
- Player can earn score by growing trees to max
- Player has a highscore
- Goal: find hotspots in the earth to place as many trees as possible, and grow them to max
- Game ends when fungal network is fully drained

expansion:
water
water is rare and precious
water grows fruits on trees and those give upgrades to the fungi, such as faster growth, more storage capacity, etc.
